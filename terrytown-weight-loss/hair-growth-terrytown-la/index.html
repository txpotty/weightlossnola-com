    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Terrytown, LA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Terrytown

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. It is driven by DHT, a hormone made from testosterone that slowly shrinks scalp follicles in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, which is why it follows a predictable pattern at the temples and crown. The two main medicines are finasteride, which cuts DHT, and minoxidil, which lengthens the growth phase. Used together, they usually beat either one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Topical minoxidil is the approved starting point. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Terrytown

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Topical minoxidil extends the growth phase and thickens individual hairs. The pairing is the standard. Some men use a compounded topical spray that combines both, which is not FDA-approved but may reduce whole-body exposure to finasteride.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. Approved for prostate enlargement, it is used off-label for hair, usually after finasteride falls short. It lingers. Its half-life is about five weeks, so screening and side effect planning take more care. Minoxidil is added for growth.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Terrytown

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Women usually start with minoxidil.

    What to Expect Over Time in Terrytown

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Results last only as long as treatment does.
