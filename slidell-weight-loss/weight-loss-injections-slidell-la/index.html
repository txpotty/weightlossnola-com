    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Slidell, LA

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Slidell

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide targets one receptor. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which may explain its larger average effect in trials.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. Diet and activity still count. Pregnancy and some thyroid cancer histories rule them out.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Slidell

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy begins at 0.25 mg and climbs every four weeks through 0.5, 1 and 1.7 mg to a maintenance dose of 2.4 mg, though some people stay at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. The first month tends to show small changes on the scale since the dose is low, while stomach upset clusters right after each shot. Large trials reported average losses near 15 percent with semaglutide 2.4 mg over 68 weeks and near 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Slidell

    Nausea is the classic one. Diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, heartburn and mild redness at the injection site are also common, especially in the days after starting or raising the dose. Smaller meals, protein first and plenty of water help most people. Less common but serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones and kidney strain from dehydration.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Your provider then reviews every medication you take, looking hardest at insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills, since tirzepatide can make oral contraceptives less reliable after dose changes. A family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 is a hard stop. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Slidell

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro contain the same active ingredients but are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes, not weight loss alone. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Availability drives some people toward them.
